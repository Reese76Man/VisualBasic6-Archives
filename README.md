# VisualBasic6-Archives
An archive of my VB6 Projects

This is the part of my life where the development skills were paying the bills!  I was hired by the Louisiana Attorney Disciplinary Board to write a case management system.  It was VB6 (client) and SQL (server) and Crystal (Reporting), which became the core of a system that I would add onto over the course of over 20 years.  It was even ported to other jurisdictions as well (Hawaii, Maine, Washington DC, etc.).  I'm sure that by now, they've all been ported to a web app or who knows what.  Those proprietary works of art won't be among anything you see here... but it was worth mentioning along with dozens of other great scripts and projects that helped a niche market's workflows become more efficient.  Outside of work, I didn't have a lot of VB6 projects, but I'll scrape together what I can and drop them here!
