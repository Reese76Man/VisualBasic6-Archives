# VisualBasic6-Archives
An archive of my VB6 Projects

This period marks part of my life where the development skills started paying the bills!  I was hired by the Louisiana Attorney Disciplinary Board to write an entire case management system.  It was VB6 (client) and SQL (server) and Crystal (Reporting), which together became the core of a system that I would spend the next 20 years continually adding onto.  It was even ported to other jurisdictions as well (Hawaii, Maine, Washington DC, etc.).  I'm sure that by now, they've all been ported to a web app or who knows what.  Those proprietary works of art won't be among anything you see here... but they were the core of my VB6 journey and were worth mentioning (along with dozens of other great scripts and projects that helped a niche market's workflows become more efficient).  Outside of the job, I didn't have a lot of VB6 projects, but I'll scrape together what I can and drop them here!  The early 2000's were the days of ActiveX and ADO.  What time to be alive!
