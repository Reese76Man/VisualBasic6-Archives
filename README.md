# VisualBasic6-Archives
An archive of my VB6 Projects

This is the part of my life where the development skills were paying the bills!  I was hired by the Louisiana Attorney Disciplinary Board to write a case management system.  It was VB6 (client) and SQL (server) and Crystal (Reporting), which became the base of a system that I added onto over the course of over 20 years.  It was ported to other jurisdictions as well (Hawaii, Maine, Washington DC, etc.).  I'm sure that by now, they've all been ported to a web app or who knows what.  Those proprietary works of art won't be among anything you see here... but it was a lot of good scripts and projects helping workflows in a niche market.  Outside of work, I didn't have a lot of VB6 projects, but I'll scrape together what I can and drop it here!
